[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

This covers:

- the curated records in `data/`: places, congregations, people, institutions, periodicals, cemeteries, sites, events, sources, Wikidata matches, and city introductions;
- the text of the site's pages;
- the files the build generates from them: JSON-LD, N-Triples, CSV/CSVW, GeoJSON, and Linked Places Format.

To credit the data, cite *Kehillot: A Digital Atlas of Jewish America, 1840–1870*, with its URL (https://judaicadh.github.io/kehillot/) and version. Each record page also gives a citation with a permanent URI.
